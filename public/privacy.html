Applicant privacy notice

Updated November 2, 2026

This notice covers the TenantFirst Management hiring application. The company’s rental application is a separate process.

Information in your application

The form asks for your name, email, optional phone number, Facebook experience, availability, work setup, and written answers. It also records your acknowledgement of the application terms.

Do not include Facebook passwords, identity documents, Social Security numbers, banking details, or payment account details. The hiring application does not request them.

How your answers are used and delivered

TenantFirst uses your answers to review your application and contact you about the role. Katherine Riordan is the hiring contact.

Applications are sent through Formspree, the form-processing service used by this site. Formspree processes the submission for the configured TenantFirst account. See Formspree’s privacy policy for information about its processing.

If this site is served with the applicant portal enabled, an accepted application is also stored in the portal database and can be retrieved using its private access key. On static hosting, accepted submissions are delivered to Formspree without creating a portal record.

While you complete the form

Your unsubmitted answers are held in the open page and are lost when it is reloaded or closed. Your light or dark theme preference is saved in your browser. If the applicant portal issues an access key, it is kept in session storage for the current browser tab. Keep that key private.

Questions, corrections, and deletion requests

Contact Katherine to ask about your application information, request a correction or deletion, or ask how long your submitted application will be retained. Include the email address used to apply; do not send identity documents or financial details with your request.
